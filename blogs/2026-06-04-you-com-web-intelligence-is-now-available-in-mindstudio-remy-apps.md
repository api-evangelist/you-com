AI-powered apps have a freshness problem: LLMs only know what they were trained on, leaving out yesterday's breaking news and real-time data. To compensate, developers build retrieval layers — and You.com Web Intelligence is now available in MindStudio's Remy Apps to solve exactly this. The integration allows MindStudio developers to ground their AI apps in live web data through the You.com Search API.
